Three steps, about three minutes.

  1. Make your first save

    Add the Scoop button to Chrome. Then open any page you like, click the button, and add a line on why you are saving it.

    No extension? Paste a link instead
    The button reads a page only when you click it. It can add to your library and can never read it.
    Waiting for your first save

  2. Connect your agent

    Scoop does no thinking of its own. Your agent tags your saves and pulls from them, so connect the one you use.

    Your browser will open so you can sign in. Each agent gets its own access, and you can end it at any time.

  3. Ask it to tag your save

    Your save has no tags yet, because Scoop never tags anything itself. Say this to :

    Tag the untagged saves in my Scoop library.

    It will ask you before it changes anything else, and you can undo whatever it does.

    Waiting for the first tag
