Pick a curve on the right and drag the two handles to change it. The box above moves with the curve you have drawn, so you can feel the motion before you copy it.

Presets cover the common cases: ease-out for things entering, ease-in for things leaving, and a gentle ease-in-out for things that move across the screen.
